Why we watch this

This was the earliest signal in the dot-com bust. Lucent's receivables (+41-64%) and inventory (+45-74%) outgrew sales (+6-33%) for eight straight quarters and went 89 to 102 days — all computable from the filings, 6-18 months before the share price peaked. Write-downs, downgrades and cuts came 9-28 months after it.

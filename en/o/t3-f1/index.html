2026-08-17US investment-grade issuance reached $1,681bn year to date, up 26.9%, a third consecutive monthly record. AI-related issuance by hyperscalers was about $225bn, up 973.7%. That is 13.4% of all IG issuance but roughly 57% of the market's year-on-year increase.報道（2026-08-17）。※IG発行総額とAI関連の内訳はいずれも二次。一次での確認は未了
The claim that AI bonds are crowding out Treasury demand does not hold on size: $225bn of AI issuance is about half the annual 20- and 30-year Treasury supply and far smaller than total Treasury issuance. The effect is inside the corporate bond market, where AI accounts for roughly 60% of the growth. Any competition with government bonds runs through the judgement of the same yield-sensitive buyers, not through volume.

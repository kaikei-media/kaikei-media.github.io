Why we watch this

Fibre lasts ~25 years; GPUs 3-6. A 25-year asset can wait out a demand shortfall; a 4-year asset cannot. A builder shortening its own assumed life is itself a signal (Amazon cut part of its server fleet from 6 to 5 years in January 2025, citing the pace of AI technology change, at about $0.3bn a quarter of net income). Extensions flatter reported profit (Meta to 5.5 years). Classified as leading.
